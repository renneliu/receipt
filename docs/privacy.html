Privacy Policy

ReceiptPrinter (macOS)
Effective date: July 25, 2026
Last updated: August 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the ReceiptPrinter macOS application (“App”, “we”, “us”) handles information when you use the App. ReceiptPrinter is a local utility for designing and printing thermal receipts, tickets, and related documents on your Mac.

1. Summary

  • We do not operate our own cloud backend that receives your receipt content.
  • Print jobs, templates, drafts, and settings are stored on your Mac.
  • Optional TMDB lookups may run if you provide your own API key.
  • The App does not include Gmail, Google account sign-in, advertising SDKs, or analytics SDKs.

2. Information stored on your device

Depending on how you use the App, it may store locally (typically under your user Library Application Support folder for the App):

  • Receipt / ticket templates, logos, layouts, and drafts
  • Print history and diagnostic records you generate in the App
  • Printer-related preferences and other App settings
  • Imported files you choose (for example spreadsheets, PDFs, or images), when you open or save them
  • Optional secrets you enter (such as a TMDB API key), stored in a restricted folder on your Mac with file permissions limited to your user account

These materials remain under your control on your computer. Uninstalling the App or deleting the App’s Application Support data removes that local data (subject to normal macOS backup copies you may have created).

3. Optional network features

3.1 TMDB (optional)

If you enter a TMDB API key in Settings, the App may send movie title search queries to The Movie Database to look up runtime and related metadata. Your API key is stored on your Mac. TMDB’s practices are described in TMDB’s Privacy Policy.

3.2 Printing

Print data is sent to printers you select through macOS / CUPS on your local system (or network printers you have already configured in macOS). The App requests sandbox permission to print and to access files you explicitly select.

4. Information we do not collect

The App developer does not collect through the App:

  • Advertising identifiers or cross-app tracking profiles
  • Analytics or usage telemetry sent to our servers (we do not run such servers for the App)
  • Your receipt content uploaded to us for storage or marketing
  • Email or Google account data (Gmail integration has been removed)

If you contact us by email or GitHub, we will receive whatever information you choose to include in that message.

5. Children’s privacy

The App is a business / productivity utility and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the App.

6. Data retention and deletion

Local data remains until you delete it in the App, remove the App’s support files, or erase your Mac. You can clear the TMDB API key in Settings.

7. Third-party services

Optional third-party network use is limited to:

  • TMDB (The Movie Database API), if you configure an API key
  • Apple (App Store / macOS system services such as printing and networking), as part of normal platform operation

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the App after an update means you accept the revised policy.
